Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Price Lister (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Price Lister application and website (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture from Google’s authentication service.
  • Business data you create. Products, prices, categories, invoices, client details, and any other content you enter into the Service.
  • Usage data. Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, and general usage patterns to help us improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Store and display your product catalog and invoices
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect and prevent abuse or technical issues

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure (including Firebase / Google Cloud). We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may process data on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — for secure sign-in
  • Google Cloud / Firestore — for data storage

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Contact section below).

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:
support@pricelister.app
